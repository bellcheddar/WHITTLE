Internal promiscuity controls

the ten sequences that appear under more than one target

Ten of the 2,500 sequences were submitted against more than one unrelated target. They are not deduplicated. A sequence that scores well against two unrelated targets is either polyreactive or evidence that the scoring is not target-aware, and either reading is worth knowing.
