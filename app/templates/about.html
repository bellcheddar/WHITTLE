About and methods

What WHITTLE does

WHITTLE takes 2,500 candidate nanobody sequences, 500 against each of five targets, and triages them down to a defensible shortlist of about twenty per target by running the cheapest checks first. Each candidate is scored on four axes: binding, non-specific binding, thermostability and expression. Every score is reported as a percentile within its own target, because the five targets are different sizes, different constructs and different libraries, and a pooled league table would be meaningless. Everything here is predicted or estimated from sequence and from predicted structure: nothing on this site is a measurement.

The weak axis, stated up front

Published benchmarks show that co-folding confidence does not reliably discriminate cognate from non-cognate nanobody-antigen pairs. WHITTLE is therefore strong at discarding and only suggestive about ranking. The affinity axis is a co-folding confidence prior, it is labelled as such wherever it appears, and the control-set tab reports what a scrambled-CDR negative control did to it. If that control shows the axis cannot separate real sequences from shuffled ones, that is reported as a finding and the axis is marked non-discriminating rather than quietly kept.

GLP1R is scoped to the N-terminal extracellular domain only, by explicit decision. The 7TM bundle and the extracellular loops are excluded, so a GLP1R result here cannot support any claim about binding the intact receptor in a membrane. TNFa and TL1A are co-folded as homotrimers, because the epitope commonly spans two subunits and a monomer run would be wrong rather than merely cheaper.

Axes, and what they are made of

Each axis is a weighted mean of the available component metrics, converted to a percentile within the target. An axis whose components have not been computed yet is shown as pending rather than filled with a placeholder.

Limitations

    {% for line in about.limitations or [] %}
  • {{ line }}
    • {% endfor %} {% if not about.limitations %}
  • Co-folding confidence is a weak prior for nanobody-antigen specificity and is not an affinity measurement.
  • Thermostability predictions carry wide error bars away from the training manifold.
  • The experimental-structure badge is a positive signal only; its absence says nothing about expressibility.
  • GLP1R results are scoped to the N-terminal ECD, not the intact receptor.
  • Rankings are within-target percentiles. Scores are not comparable between targets.
